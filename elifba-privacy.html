Elif-Ba — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Elif-Ba is a learning app that helps children learn the Arabic alphabet through coloring, tracing, games and songs. We designed it to be safe for children and to collect as little data as possible. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

In short: Elif-Ba has no accounts and no sign-in. Your child's learning progress and settings are stored only on the device. We do not ask for, collect, or sell personal information. Any advertising shown to children is non-personalized.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Elif-Ba does not require an account, email address, phone number, name, or age, and it does not ask children for any personal details.

Learning progress (such as which letters have been practiced, stars, badges, streaks, daily goals and app settings) is saved locally on the device using the operating system's standard storage. This information stays on the device and is not sent to us or to any server we control.

Advertising

The free version of Elif-Ba may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Because Elif-Ba is intended for children, ads are served in a non-personalized manner: they are not based on a user profile or tracking, and the app does not use the device's advertising identifier for cross-app tracking.

To serve and cap ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process limited, non-identifying technical signals (such as general device type and approximate, coarse region). Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms. Purchasing Elif-Ba Pro removes all advertisements.

Purchases

Elif-Ba offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions (Elif-Ba Pro). All payments are handled directly by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Subscriptions renew through your Apple account until cancelled, and can be managed or cancelled at any time in your device's App Store settings.

Children's privacy

Elif-Ba is made for children and follows Apple's Kids Category rules and applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA and GDPR-K). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, we do not build profiles of children, and we do not use behavioral advertising or tracking directed at children. There are no social features, no chat, and no user-generated content shared with others.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing. The only third parties involved are the service providers that make the app work — Apple (app distribution and payments) and Google AdMob (non-personalized ads in the free version) — acting under their own privacy terms.

Data retention and deletion

Because progress and settings are stored only on the device, you can remove all of this data at any time by deleting the app. Resetting progress inside the app also clears the stored learning data on the device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
