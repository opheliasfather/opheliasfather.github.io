Baby Sleeper is a calm sound and lullaby player for parents. It is made by SIX11. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

In short: No account and no sign-up. Your mixes and settings stay on your device. Ads in the free version are served by Google AdMob. Purchases go through Apple. We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for anonymous usage and stability. Buying Sleeper Pro removes ads.

What stays on your device

Saved mixes, theme, volume and timer preferences are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We do not upload your mixes or playback history.

Ads (AdMob)

If you have not bought Sleeper Pro, banner and occasional full-screen ads are shown via Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers and approximate location from IP. You can refuse tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Buying Sleeper Pro removes ads.

Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. These tools collect anonymous technical data (for example app version, device type, crash stacks). They are not used to identify you personally. See Google’s documentation for Firebase data practices.

Purchases

Sleeper Pro is a one-time App Store purchase handled by Apple. We never see your card details. We only receive Apple’s confirmation that the purchase exists.

Children

The app is operated by adults for family use. It is not directed at children under 13 as users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Questions: mehmetrasitarisu@gmail.com. Billing and refunds: Apple.
