Umbra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026 · SIX11

Umbra is a wallpaper app. It has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. Everything you do in it stays on your iPhone, apart from the advertising described below.

In short: No account and no sign-up. Favourites, style choices and unlocks stay on your device. Ads in the free version are served by Google AdMob. Purchases go through Apple. There is no analytics SDK in this app. Buying Umbra Pro removes every ad.

What stays on your device

Your favourites, the styles you pick during setup, which wallpapers you have opened, the wallpapers you unlocked by watching an ad, and your save-quality choice are stored on this iPhone using the system's own storage. They are never uploaded, and deleting the app deletes them.

Photos

Umbra asks for permission to add to your photo library the first time you save a wallpaper, and not before. The permission is add-only: the app cannot see, read or change any photo you already have.

Ads (AdMob)

Free use is paid for by ads served through Google AdMob. Google receives your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about the ads you are shown, and uses it to select ads and to measure them. Google's own privacy policy governs that data: policies.google.com/privacy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any personalised ad is requested, through Google's certified consent form. On iOS you are also asked separately whether the app may track you — you can change that any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining either is fine: the app works exactly the same, the ads are simply less relevant. Buying Umbra Pro removes every ad, and with it everything described in this section.

Purchases

Umbra Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether the purchase exists. Your name, card and billing details are never seen by this app.

Analytics

There is no analytics SDK in this app. Nothing about your use of it is collected or sent anywhere by us. Apple may share aggregate, anonymised crash and usage statistics with the developer if you have opted into that in iOS settings.

Children

Umbra is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published here and the date at the top moves.
